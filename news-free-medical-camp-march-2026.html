Free Medical Camp – 8th March 2026

Organised by Dr.Brindha Skin & Hair Clinic at No.5, Highways Colony, Subramaniapuram, Ponmalaipatti Road, Trichy

On the occasion of International Women's Day – 8th March 2026, Dr.Brindha Skin & Hair Clinic was proud to host a Free Medical Camp for the residents of Highways Colony, Subramaniapuram, Trichy. The camp was held at No.5, Highways Colony, Ponmalaipatti Road, Trichy, and was open to all members of the local community.

Good skin health is not a luxury — it is a basic need. Our goal was to bring expert dermatology care directly to the doorstep of every family in the neighbourhood, completely free of charge.

– Dr. Brindha Thangraj, MBBS, M.D. Dermatology

About the Camp

The free medical camp offered complimentary skin and hair consultations by Dr. Brindha Thangraj — a qualified Dermatologist and Cosmetologist with over a decade of clinical experience. Patients from all age groups attended the camp, including children, adults, and the elderly, making it a truly inclusive community healthcare event.

Common skin concerns such as acne, pigmentation, melasma, dandruff, hair fall, fungal infections, and eczema were screened and addressed. Personalised treatment advice and prescriptions were provided on the spot. Attendees also received awareness material on preventive skin care, sun protection, and healthy hair habits.

Impact & Highlights

The response from the community was overwhelming. Dozens of families came forward to avail the free consultation, many of whom had long been struggling with skin or hair conditions without access to specialist care. The camp created an opportunity for early diagnosis and timely guidance, which can make a significant difference to patient outcomes.

The event also helped raise awareness about the importance of seeing a qualified dermatologist rather than self-medicating or relying on over-the-counter products for complex skin conditions.

Event Details
  • Date: 8th March 2026 (International Women's Day)
  • Venue: No.5, Highways Colony, Subramaniapuram, Ponmalaipatti Road, Trichy
  • Conducted by: Dr. Brindha Thangraj, MBBS, M.D. Dermatology
  • Services: Free Skin & Hair Consultation, Prescription, Health Awareness
  • Beneficiaries: Residents of Highways Colony & surrounding areas

Dr.Brindha Skin & Hair Clinic remains committed to giving back to the community and will continue to organise such outreach events. We believe that accessible, expert dermatological care can transform lives, and every initiative like this is a step closer to that vision.

We thank all the residents who attended the camp, the volunteers who helped organise the event, and the entire team at Dr.Brindha Skin & Hair Clinic for making this day a great success.
